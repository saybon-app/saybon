[warmly] Welcome back. Today you learn two tiny words that unlock a lot of French. Je suis, which means I am. And tu es, which means you are.

[short pause]

[warmly] Listen first. Je suis content. I am happy.

[pause]

[calmly] Je suis content.

[pause]

[warmly] Now you. Say it with me. Je suis content.

[short pause]

[calmly] Je suis content.

[your turn]

[warmly] Very good.

[short pause]

[warmly] Now the other one. Tu es content. You are happy.

[short pause]

[calmly] Tu es content.

[your turn]

[warmly] Very good.

[short pause]

[warmly] Here is the nice part. A question is just the same words said with a rising voice. Tu es content ?

[short pause]

[calmly] Tu es content ?

[your turn]

[warmly] And you answer. Oui, je suis content.

[short pause]

[calmly] Oui, je suis content.

[your turn]

[warmly] Very good.

[short pause]

[warmly] Now a little conversation. I will ask. You answer, and give your own name after je suis. Ready? Bonjour !

[short pause]

[calmly] Bonjour !

[your turn]

[warmly] Tu es content ?

[short pause]

[calmly] Tu es content ?

[your turn]

[warmly] Lovely. Je suis content aussi. That means, I am happy too.

[short pause]

[warmly] Well done. You can now say who you are, and ask someone how they are. Next time, we'll use these words with more people.
