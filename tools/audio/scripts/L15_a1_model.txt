[warmly] Listen to this dialogue. You talk about the weather with a neighbour.

[pause]

[calmly] Quel temps fait-il aujourd'hui ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Il fait beau et chaud.

[short pause]

[calmly] Et demain ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Demain, il va pleuvoir.

[short pause]

[calmly] Vous aimez la pluie ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Non, je préfère le soleil.

[short pause]

[calmly] Moi aussi. Il fait froid en hiver ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Oui, il fait très froid en hiver.

[short pause]

[calmly] Alors, bon week-end !

[pause]

[warmly] That's the conversation. I'm going to teach you how to have it, and at the end of this lesson, you'll have it with me.
