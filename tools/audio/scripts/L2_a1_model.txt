[warmly] Listen to this dialogue. You are waiting at the station for a friend. A woman asks you about him.

[pause]

[calmly] Excusez-moi, vous attendez quelqu'un ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Oui, j'attends mon ami Paul.

[short pause]

[calmly] Il est comment, votre ami ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Il est grand, et il a les cheveux noirs.

[short pause]

[calmly] Il porte des lunettes ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Oui, il a des lunettes. Il est très sympa.

[short pause]

[calmly] Et vous, vous êtes comment ?

[short pause]

[as:Roger] [calmly] Moi, je suis petit, et j'ai les yeux verts.

[short pause]

[calmly] Ah, le voilà ! Bonne journée.

[pause]

[warmly] That's the conversation. I'm going to teach you how to have it, and at the end of this lesson, you'll have it with me.
