SayBon Confidentiality & NDA Terms

By accepting this job, you agree to keep everything you access through it strictly confidential. This includes the source document, the client's identity and information, your translation, any files provided, and any instructions or communications related to this assignment.

You must not share this material with anyone else, upload it to any unauthorized platform, use it for personal purposes, publish or distribute it, or contact the client directly unless SayBon expressly authorizes it. You must not share another participant's work, and you must not use another participant's submission in preparing your own.

This obligation continues after the job is completed and after your participation in it has ended.

Violating these confidentiality terms may result in immediate removal from the SayBon translator program, forfeiture of payment for this and any other pending assignments, and disqualification from future jobs. Serious or repeated violations may also lead to further legal consequences.

By accepting this job, you also confirm that the work you submit reflects your own genuine review, judgment, and effort - not simply copied from another source or submitted without meaningful review.
