SayBon Translator Pool

Join a vetted network of professional translators working on real client documents - with clear standards, fair competition, and confidentiality built into every job.
Language Requirements

Every translator in the pool works fluently in both French and English, with the writing precision that professional documents demand.

  • Proficiency in both French and English is required
  • Strong written command of both languages, not just conversational fluency
  • Prior experience translating professional-grade documents is expected
Professional Document Experience

You should be comfortable handling the kind of documents real clients actually submit:

  • Legal documents
  • Corporate reports
  • Contracts
  • Academic papers
  • Technical documentation
  • Business communications
Work Stays Inside the Platform

All translation work happens directly inside the SayBon workspace - nothing leaves the platform.

  • Documents cannot be downloaded
  • Documents cannot be copied outside the platform
  • Documents cannot be stored locally
  • Documents cannot be transferred into external software
This keeps client documents contained and protected at every stage of the job.
Non-Disclosure Agreements

Every job starts with a confidentiality agreement - no exceptions.

  • An NDA must be signed before you can access any job
  • You may be asked to sign more than one NDA, depending on the job type
  • Everything handled through SayBon is treated as confidential
Violating confidentiality results in immediate removal from the translator pool.
Job Competition Model

Every new job on the board opens as a race for a limited number of seats.

  • The first three translators to accept a job secure a place in it
  • Once three have accepted, the job closes to further participants
  • The board shows clearly when a job has reached capacity

From there, the competition shifts from speed of acceptance to speed and quality of delivery.

Quality Evaluation

Every submission goes through quality review before it counts.

  • The minimum passing score is 85%
  • Work that falls short is rejected
  • You'll receive a clear explanation if your submission doesn't pass

Review focuses on the accuracy and quality of the final translation you deliver.

Payment Distribution

Payment is determined by submission order and passing quality review.

  • First translator to submit approved work: 60% payment
  • Second translator to pass quality review: 10% payment
  • Third translator to pass quality review: 10% payment

If the first submission doesn't pass quality review, no payment is issued for it.

Payment Timeline

Once your work passes quality review, payment appears in your Wallet on the Job Board right away. It becomes available to cash out 3 business days after approval, and is paid out directly to your bank account or mobile money once your available balance reaches $10, processed in weekly batches.

Registration Requirements

Joining the pool starts with a professional translation test.

  • The test is completed entirely inside the SayBon platform
  • It cannot be copied or exported
  • You'll need to provide national identification
  • Professional certifications may be requested

Identity verification is required before you're approved into the pool.

Work Availability

Joining the pool doesn't guarantee a fixed volume of work. What's available to you depends on:

  • Client demand
  • Demand for your specific language pair
  • Your track record and performance
  • Competition from other translators in the pool
