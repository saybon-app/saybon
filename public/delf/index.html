What is the DELF?

The DELF (Diplôme d'Études en Langue Française) is an official French proficiency diploma, recognized worldwide and valid for life. Passing it can open doors — for study, work, or personal milestones. SayBon helps you walk into the real exam prepared, not guessing.

Four skills, properly tested

Listening

Understand spoken French in real, natural contexts.

Reading

Comprehend written passages with confidence.

Writing

Express yourself clearly in structured written French.

Speaking

Speak naturally, with real feedback on how you sound.

Your Placement Is a Mirror, Not a Gate

Before you begin, you'll take a mock placement test — a shortened version of the real exam covering all four skills. It's not there to judge you. It's there to show you, honestly, where you're starting from.

Your placement score is saved to your DELF dashboard as your baseline. From there, every test score you earn throughout your prep — all ten of them — gets added right alongside it, so you can see your own progress laid out clearly, from day one to your last practice test.

You Choose Your Level. Always.

You're free to test into whichever level you believe fits you. Once we've scored your placement, we'll tell you — clearly and honestly — what our findings suggest. If our recommendation differs from your choice, that's completely fine. You decide.

We'll still prepare you at whatever level you choose. We just want you to walk in with your eyes open: a level far above where you're starting can feel like a steep climb, and one far below can feel like it's not challenging you. Our job is to tell you what we see — the choice is always yours.
