The DELF, Explained

The DELF (Diplôme d'Études en Langue Française) is an official French proficiency diploma — recognized internationally, and valid for life. Earning it can open real doors, for study, work, or a personal milestone. SayBon exists to help you walk into that exam prepared, not guessing.

Four Skills. One Real Exam.

Listening

Understand spoken French in real, natural contexts.

Reading

Comprehend written passages with confidence.

Writing

Express yourself clearly in structured written French.

Speaking

Speak naturally, with real feedback on how you sound.

Every Age, Every Level

DELF isn't one-size-fits-all, and neither is SayBon's prep. We support two youth tracks and the full adult track:

DELF Prim · ages 8–12

Covers A1.1, A1, and A2 — the earliest levels, built for younger learners just beginning their French journey.

DELF Junior · ages 13–17

Covers A1 through B2 — the same content as the adult exam, adapted with themes and material suited to teenagers.

DELF Tout Public · adults

Covers A1 through C1 — the standard adult track, for learners at any stage of their French.

Your Placement Is a Mirror, Not a Gate

Before you begin, you'll take a mock placement test — a shortened version of the real exam, covering all four skills. It isn't there to judge you. It's there to show you, honestly, where you're starting from.

Your placement score is saved to your DELF dashboard as your baseline. From there, every test score you earn throughout your preparation — all ten of them — is added right alongside it, so your progress is laid out clearly, from day one to your very last practice test.

You Choose Your Level. Always.

You're free to test into whichever level you believe fits you. Once we've scored your placement, we'll share our findings clearly and honestly. If our recommendation differs from your choice, that's entirely fine — you decide.

We'll prepare you at whatever level you choose. We simply want you to walk in with your eyes open: a level far above where you're starting can feel like a steep climb, and one far below may not challenge you at all. Our role is to tell you what we see. The choice is always yours.

How Your Preparation Actually Works

Each level follows a simple rhythm: four lessons, then a test. Every lesson focuses on a single skill — Listening, then Reading, then Writing, then Speaking — before a practice test brings it together. This full round repeats ten times per level.

1

Four focused lessons

Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking — one at a time, building real skill in each area.

2

One practice test

Every batch of four lessons ends with a real test, scored and added to your dashboard.

3

Repeated ten times

By the end, you'll have completed forty lessons and ten full tests — a genuinely thorough preparation.

4

Skip if you're confident

Feeling strong in a skill? You're free to skip a lesson. We'll always share our recommendation first — but the choice stays yours.
