SayBon

Investor Business Plan

Learn French. Feel French. Use French.

1. Executive Summary

SayBon is a Ghanaian-built, unified French-learning ecosystem - not a single app, but seven interconnected products working together: structured CEFR-aligned lessons with live pronunciation scoring, gamified speaking practice, DELF exam preparation, an original educational music catalog, a two-sided professional translation marketplace, and a community chat system, all sitting on top of real operational infrastructure most companies at this stage don't yet have.

Unlike single-purpose language apps, SayBon is designed so that learning, certification, practice, and real-world language use reinforce each other inside one platform - reducing the fragmentation that causes most learners to give up.

2. The Global Problem

  • High cost of quality French education, especially outside major Western markets
  • Learner dropout rates of 80-90% are common across traditional language platforms
  • DELF exam preparation is fragmented, expensive, and rarely tailored to a learner's actual level
  • Most platforms teach language in isolation, with no real bridge from lessons to real-world speaking confidence

3. The SayBon Platform

SayBon is built as a genuine ecosystem, with each product reinforcing the others:

Levels - Structured CEFR Learning

Seven distinct learning tracks spanning A0 through C1, each with its own real visual identity, working through a consistent teach-practice-apply lesson structure: learners are taught new material, then record themselves speaking and receive live pronunciation scoring powered by Azure Speech AI, then apply what they've learned in a realistic scenario. Progression unlocks sequentially, so learners build real competence rather than skipping ahead.

Talkletics - Gamified Speaking Practice

A companion pronunciation game with a world-map progression, guided by an interactive character, giving learners a lower-pressure space to build speaking confidence outside the structured curriculum.

DELF Exam Preparation

A genuine placement-to-certification pathway: a full diagnostic placement test determines a learner's real level, followed by structured, level-specific preparation with tracked progress toward the internationally recognized DELF certification.

SayBon Music

An original, wholly-owned catalog of educational music spanning multiple genres, designed to help vocabulary and pronunciation stick through the same repetition and emotional engagement that makes songs memorable in any language.

Translation Services - A Real Two-Sided Marketplace

A working marketplace connecting clients with verified, tested French-English translators, supported by an AI-assisted draft workflow to accelerate turnaround, with real payment processing already live and operational.

Community & Support

An integrated chat system giving learners a space to practice and connect, with content moderation built in from the start.

4. Technology & Differentiation

  • Live AI pronunciation assessment - every speaking exercise across Levels, DELF, and Talkletics is scored in real time using Azure Speech, not just self-reported or peer-reviewed
  • One ecosystem, not a bundle - learning, certification prep, gamified practice, and real-world application are built as one connected product, not separate apps stitched together
  • Real operational maturity for this stage - SayBon already runs on a genuine internal business operations suite: live financial tracking, real-time payment verification, cap table management, and document infrastructure - the kind of operational backbone most companies build well after their first funding round, not before it
