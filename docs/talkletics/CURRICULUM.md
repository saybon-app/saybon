# Talkletics curriculum (A0 to C2)

Speaking only. The learner speaks, the platform grades, and they advance at the pass mark. 7 levels, 49 games, 7 graduation challenges.

## Pre-A1 (A0) - Ma Maison (My House)
The learner uses isolated words, familiar names and very short set phrases, with lots of repetition and visual support.

**Pass mark:** games 55%, graduation 60% · **Answer length:** 1-8 s · **Focus:** Greetings, name, how are you, home vocabulary, family (ma/mon), oui/non, j'ai / je m'appelle

**The learner can:**
- Greet people and say goodbye politely
- Say their name and ask someone's name
- Name everyday objects and rooms in the home
- Name close family members and answer a yes/no question about them

**Games**

| # | Game | Type | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Salutations (Greetings) | Repeat after me | 8 |
| 2 | Je me présente (Introducing myself) | Repeat after me | 6 |
| 3 | Dans la maison (In the house) | Say what you see | 7 |
| 4 | Ma famille (My family) | Say what you see | 7 |
| 🎓 | Bienvenue chez moi (Welcome to my home) | Role-play | 5 |

**Graduating from Pre-A1 (A0) means:** You can greet a neighbour, say who you are, say where you live and whether you have children, and say goodbye, using short phrases, even if you need slow speech and repetition.

## A1 - La Pêche d'Or (The Golden Peach (café and grocery))
The learner interacts in a simple way with slow, clear speech, using familiar everyday expressions and basic sentences about needs, likes and daily routine.

**Pass mark:** games 60%, graduation 65% · **Answer length:** 2-12 s · **Focus:** Numbers and prices, food and drink, je voudrais / je prends, j'aime / je n'aime pas, present tense of everyday verbs, telling the time

**The learner can:**
- Count and say prices
- Name common food and drinks
- Order something politely and pay
- Say what they like and don't like
- Describe a simple daily routine with times

**Games**

| # | Game | Type | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Les nombres et les prix (Numbers and prices) | Say what you see | 7 |
| 2 | Boissons et gourmandises (Drinks and treats) | Say what you see | 7 |
| 3 | Je voudrais... (I would like...) | Role-play | 7 |
| 4 | J'aime, je n'aime pas (Likes and dislikes) | Answer the question | 7 |
| 5 | Ma journée (My day) | Describe | 6 |
| 🎓 | Le client du jour (Customer of the day) | Role-play | 6 |

**Graduating from A1 means:** You can walk into a café, get a table, order food and drink, say what you like, and pay, using simple sentences. The other person may need to speak slowly.

## A2 - Le Restaurant (The Restaurant)
The learner handles simple, routine exchanges and describes things and past events in a series of short sentences.

**Pass mark:** games 63%, graduation 68% · **Answer length:** 4-20 s · **Focus:** Booking, ordering, preferences with parce que, passé composé (avoir / être), polite requests (pourriez-vous...)

**The learner can:**
- Book a table by phone and give the details
- Order a full meal and react to what the waiter asks
- Say what they prefer and why, using parce que
- Say what happened yesterday in the passé composé
- Politely report a problem and ask for a fix

**Games**

| # | Game | Type | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Réserver une table (Booking a table) | Role-play | 6 |
| 2 | Lire la carte (Reading the menu) | Say what you see | 6 |
| 3 | Passer commande (Placing your order) | Role-play | 6 |
| 4 | Goûts et préférences (Tastes and preferences) | Give your opinion | 6 |
| 5 | Hier soir (Last night) | Describe | 6 |
| 6 | Un petit problème (A small problem) | Role-play | 6 |
| 🎓 | Soirée au Restaurant (An evening at the restaurant) | Role-play | 6 |

**Graduating from A2 means:** You can book, order, solve a small problem and pay in a restaurant, and tell someone what you ate last night, using short connected sentences with only occasional hesitation.

## B1 - La Gare et l'Hôtel (The Station and the Hotel)
The learner copes with most travel situations, enters conversations on familiar topics without preparation, and links sentences to describe experiences, plans and opinions.

**Pass mark:** games 66%, graduation 71% · **Answer length:** 15-40 s · **Focus:** Past narration, future and conditional, directions, connectors (d'abord, ensuite, mais, parce que, donc), polite requests and complaints

**The learner can:**
- Buy tickets and handle changes or delays
- Give and follow directions
- Check in at a hotel and solve problems
- Narrate a past trip (passé composé and imparfait)
- Talk about plans, hopes and dreams (futur, conditionnel)
- Give an opinion and support it with two reasons

**Games**

| # | Game | Type | Items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Acheter un billet (Buying a ticket) | Role-play | 6 |
| 2 | Itinéraires (Giving directions) | Describe | 6 |
| 3 | À l'hôtel (At the hotel) | Role-play | 6 |
| 4 | Mes voyages (My travels) | Tell a story | 6 |
| 5 | Projets de voyage (Travel plans) | Give your opinion | 6 |
| 6 | Donner son avis (Giving your opinion) | Give your opinion | 6 |
| 7 | Imprévus (When things go wrong) | Role-play | 6 |
| 🎓 | Voyage raté (A trip gone wrong) | Role-play | 5 |

**Graduating from B1 means:** You can deal with a travel disaster in French: explain what happened, propose and justify a solution, tell a related story and give advice, speaking for 30 to 40 seconds at a time with some hesitation but clear organisation.

## B2 - Le Bureau (The Office)
The learner interacts with fluency and spontaneity, gives clear detailed descriptions, and argues a viewpoint by weighing advantages and disadvantages.

**Pass mark:** games 70%, graduation 75% · **Answer length:** 20-55 s · **Focus:** Argument structure, concession, hypothesis (si + conditionnel), passive and nominalisation, trend vocabulary, professional register (vous), diplomatic phrasing

**The learner can:**
- Present themselves and their experience in an interview
- Take part in a meeting: agree, disagree and propose, with nuance
- Negotiate a request and agree a compromise
- Describe charts and trends with precise vocabulary
- Build a structured argument with concession and conclusion
- Summarise a spoken text in their own words

**Games**

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Entretien d'embauche (Job interview) | Role-play | 5 |
| 2 | Mon métier (My job) | Describe | 5 |
| 3 | En réunion (In a meeting) | Role-play | 5 |
| 4 | Négocier (Negotiating) | Negotiate | 5 |
| 5 | Décrire un graphique (Describing a chart) | Describe | 5 |
| 6 | Argumenter (Arguing a case) | Debate | 5 |
| 7 | Gérer un conflit (Handling conflict) | Role-play | 5 |
| 8 | Résumer (Summarising) | Summarise | 5 |
| 🎓 | Présentation défendue (Presentation and defence) | Present | 4 |

**Graduating from B2 means:** You can present a topic from a short document, defend your viewpoint against objections and conclude convincingly, speaking fluently for up to a minute at a time with good organisation and few errors that do not block meaning.

## C1 - La Conférence (The Conference)
The learner expresses ideas fluently and spontaneously, structures long discourse on complex subjects and adapts register to the audience.

**Pass mark:** games 73%, graduation 78% · **Answer length:** 30-55 s · **Focus:** Discourse markers, subjunctive, nominal style, concession and opposition, register control, rhetorical devices, summary and synthesis

**The learner can:**
- Open and structure a talk with clear signposting
- Present and interpret data with nuance
- Argue for and against a position, refuting objections
- Explain a complex idea simply
- Handle difficult questions from an audience
- Adapt register and tell a story with impact

**Graduating from C1 means:** You can deliver a structured talk in several parts and answer unexpected questions, with a wide vocabulary, flexible grammar and a clear organising thread.

## C2 - Le Grand Salon (The Grand Salon)
The learner expresses themselves spontaneously, very fluently and precisely, differentiating fine shades of meaning, even in the most complex situations.

**Pass mark:** games 76%, graduation 81% · **Answer length:** 35-55 s · **Focus:** Rhetoric, irony, idioms, fine lexical nuance, complex syntax, register mastery, spontaneity

**The learner can:**
- Grasp and play with implicit meaning, irony and humour
- Use idioms and figurative language accurately
- Persuade, mediate and speak in public with rhetorical skill
- Switch register and perspective effortlessly
- Improvise on any topic with structure and wit
- Converse with experts at a high conceptual level

**Graduating from C2 means:** You can take part in a high-level debate: argue, rebut, mediate and conclude with elegance, precision and natural flow.
